# Origin Story

When I was new to the United States, I went to my first specialist appointment alone. I arrived holding a stack of papers, unsure which ones mattered. At the front desk, I kept searching through them and asking the receptionist: *What should I bring? Are these all the documents you need?*

Nobody had told me beforehand to bring blood test results or reports from my previous doctor. When they asked whether I had any, I didn't share them. I was afraid that if something was missing, they would reschedule my appointment, and I had already waited a long time for it.

By the time I saw the doctor, my mind was still on the paperwork. I didn't feel confident, and I felt the doctor didn't give me much attention. I had questions about my health, but I didn't ask them. All my energy had gone into making sure I had the right documents.

Later, I realized I wasn't the only one. Many people forget what the doctor's office asked them to bring, and when that happens, they often have to reschedule and wait again. That experience is why I'm building CarePath AI.
